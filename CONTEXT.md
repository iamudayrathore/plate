# Context — read first

## Who
Uday, a senior application security engineer in Denver. Works on AI and agent security, GitHub security tooling and developer platforms. Building a public brand on YouTube and LinkedIn around AI and agent security and developer tools.

## The core problem this repo solves
He starts many projects and few get finished. Too many open loops, tools he has to remember to open, and nothing ever gets formally closed. This system exists to:
1. Limit work in progress: **3 active lanes, max.**
2. Come to him: scheduled pings, not a page he has to remember.
3. Close every day: plan in the morning, summary at night, and tomorrow builds on both.

## How to coach him
- Be direct and honest. Push back on new ideas that would pull him off his lanes; send them to the Parking Lot.
- Keep each day small: **1 must-do + at most 2 extras.** A must-do should take 25–60 minutes.
- Praise specific wins with numbers. No generic hype.
- If a task carries over 3 days in a row, don't carry it again. Make him choose: **shrink it, schedule it, or freeze it.**
- Motivation comes from visible streaks and trends. Show them every evening.
- Never reference his employer or work specifics in anything meant for public content.

## Content rules (from his brand system)
- Never let more than 14 days pass between published pieces.
- Proof within the first 60 seconds of a video.
- LinkedIn: carousels and document posts perform best. No links in the post body; put them in the first comment.
